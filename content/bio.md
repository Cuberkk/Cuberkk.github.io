I am a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at [Case Western Reserve University (CWRU)](https://case.edu/), advised by [Prof. Zonghe Chua](https://case.edu/engineering/about/faculty-and-staff-directory/zonghe-chua) in the [Enhanced Robotic Interfaces & Experiences (ERIE) Lab](https://enhanced-telerobotics.github.io/index.html).

My current research focuses on **differentiable physical simulation for soft robotic grippers**. My broader interests include vision-based sensing, soft haptic actuators, soft robotic grippers, physical simulation, and machine learning.

I received my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from CWRU, also advised by Prof. Chua, and my B.Eng. in Mechanical Design, Manufacturing and Automation from [Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST)](https://english.hust.edu.cn/).
