CWRU and HFI use their official compact website icons. IEEE uses the master logo provided by its Bangalore Section. The HUST image is from a secondary source and was visually compared with the university's [official emblem specification](https://vi.hust.edu.cn/jcbf/bzgf/xhgf.htm); the official high-resolution attachment requires a CAPTCHA.

Mappings are declared with `logo` in `content/experience.toml`: university entries use their university's mark; IEEE reviewing uses IEEE; the Human Fusions open-house presentation uses HFI. CWRU teaching, lab outreach, internal reviewing, and the award use CWRU. These marks identify the institutions attached to the stated experiences.
